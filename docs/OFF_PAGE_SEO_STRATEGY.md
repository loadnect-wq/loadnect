# Off-page SEO — Hallnect

Off-page work is the slowest and most easily wasted part of SEO, and it is the part where buying shortcuts does real, lasting damage. This is scoped to what a one-venue marketplace in Madurai can honestly do.

---

## 1. Sequence matters

Links to a site with one venue mostly go to waste — there is nothing behind the front door. The order that works:

1. **Verify the GBP** (`LOCAL_SEO_SETUP.md`). One verified local entity beats a dozen directory links.
2. **Sign venues.** Each one is a page worth linking to, and an owner with a reason to link.
3. **Then** pursue the rest of this document.

Doing 3 before 2 is how link-building budgets disappear.

## 2. What each venue owner can legitimately give

This is the most natural link source Hallnect has, and it is free.

- **Their own Google Business Profile** — the venue's own verified profile can list `hallnect.com/halls/<slug>` under website or services. A genuine citation from a relevant local entity.
- **Their own site or Facebook page** — "Book us on Hallnect" with a link to their listing.
- **Their WhatsApp Business catalogue** — not a link Google counts, but real referral traffic, which is the point.

Ask at onboarding, while they are motivated. Never require it as a condition of listing — a link given under obligation is both lower quality and against Google's guidelines on link schemes.

## 3. Directories and citations

Free, relevant, and with the exact NAP from `LOCAL_SEO_SETUP.md`:

- Justdial — highest local traffic in India
- Sulekha, IndiaMART — wedding services
- WeddingWire India, WedMeGood — where couples actually search
- Bing Places — import from GBP after verification

Consistency is worth more than volume. Six identical listings beat thirty with three different phone numbers.

## 4. Partnerships that are also a business

The wedding supply chain in Madurai is a small, connected market: photographers, decorators, caterers, mehendi artists, event planners, transport. Each of them:

- talks to the same couples, earlier in the journey
- often has a website with a "vendors we work with" page
- benefits genuinely from being able to point clients at halls

A reciprocal listing that reflects a real working relationship is legitimate. A link exchange for its own sake is a link scheme. The distinction is whether the relationship exists.

## 5. Local press and community

- Tamil Nadu wedding blogs and local lifestyle sites — worth a pitch only once there is something to write about. "A new marketplace with one venue" is not a story; "how hall pricing works in Madurai, with real numbers" might be.
- Local business associations and chambers of commerce — often have member directories.
- College and community noticeboards where event venues are genuinely relevant.

## 6. Content that earns links, later

The strongest asset this business could eventually publish is **data nobody else has**: real hall pricing by capacity and season in Madurai, drawn from actual listings. That is the kind of thing local journalists and wedding blogs cite.

It requires enough listings for the numbers to be meaningful and non-identifying. Do not publish it from one venue — it would be a single hall's tariff presented as market data, which is both useless and misleading.

## 7. Absolutely not

Per the brief, and because every one of these is a real risk to a young domain:

**Paid link packages · PBNs · automated backlink submission · link farms · fake reviews · fake or duplicate business profiles · comment and forum spam · AI-generated satellite sites · cloaking · hidden text · doorway pages.**

Two specific to this project:

- **Do not create Google Business Profiles for venues Hallnect does not own.** It violates Google's guidelines and risks the Hallnect profile itself — the one asset that matters most here.
- **Do not seed reviews.** Not from staff, not from family, not incentivised, and no review-gating. With zero completed bookings there is nothing to ask for yet; the first genuine review comes after the first genuine booking.

## 8. Measuring

Search Console → **Links** shows external links Google has found. Expect near-zero for months; that is normal and not a reason to buy any.

The honest measure of off-page work at this stage is **referral sessions in GA4** — did a real person arrive from that partner, that directory, that owner's page? A link that sends nobody is worth little regardless of what a toolbar metric says.
